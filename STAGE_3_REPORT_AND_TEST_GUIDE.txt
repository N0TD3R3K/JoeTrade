WHAT WAS FINISHED OR CORRECTED
1. Durable exchange-to-bank delivery
   Income batches are now prepared before bank writes, committed atomically on
   the bank, and acknowledged on the exchange through the existing recovery
   mechanism. A lost response retains a retryable prepared operation instead of
   losing evidence or leaving an unrecoverable planning action. Multiple batches
   with identical timestamps drain without waiting for another clock bucket.
   Full settlement evidence is immutable; the bank retains compact proof hashes
   and references. Manual delivery retries process up to fifty income events;
   larger queues continue on later scheduled runs. Rate-event receipt and news
   creation hold the market mutex so an engine tick cannot consume a new signal
   halfway through its stock list.

2. Scheduled banking work and noon reconciliation
   Durable cursors rotate renewals, accrual, expiry, payroll, assessments and
   collections past deferred items. Five blocked companies/members no longer
   prevent later due items from running. Collection checks all latest assessments,
   independent of the console's recent-results display limit.
   Read-only noon capture works before cash policy approval. Exchange scheduled
   recovery automatically attempts the guarded shared snapshot after New York
   noon. A reconciled current-date observation is not recaptured every minute.
   Actual capture time stays visible; missed historical noon values are not made up.

3. Subscription and tax corrections
   Retry payment is limited to past-due subscriptions; it cannot charge an active
   subscription early. Repeated unfunded retries reuse the original past-due event
   without conflicting with its unique delivery reference.
   Tax collection/refund checks the current income IDs, unresolved basis holds
   and exchange cutoff. Late evidence makes an old bill stale until an attributed
   revision is created. Redistribution also accounts for possible tax-refund
   obligations rather than spending cash owed back to members.

4. Shared account UI
   Consented payments and loan actions refresh JoeSE's cash and liabilities
   immediately without replacing the entire page. Connected net position now
   subtracts active bank loan principal plus accrued interest. Unaccepted offers
   create no liability. Banking actions refresh their own products and balances.
   Banking/JOESE links use local addresses during this preview and carry Alice/Bob
   selection between sites. Production links retain joe-univer.se hostnames.
   The bank overview now links to the connected exchange. The guide and console
   market-health wording have been updated to describe the implemented features.

5. Local preview runner
   From the JoeTrade root, npm run dev:all starts all three websites, two APIs,
   the price engine and local scheduled work. It reuses an existing owned group,
   refuses occupied ports, and never seeds, migrates or approves policy.
   npm run stop:local stops only the recorded owned preview group.
   Local jobs run once per minute while the preview is running. Price ticks keep
   their hourly keys and New York 14:00–22:00 session; prices are not forced.
   Test order entry remains open locally. Production schedule/demo settings are
   unchanged. Keep the runner's terminal/session running during testing.
