3. DESIGN DEFAULTS RECORDED FOR STAGE 2

The complete matrix is docs/stage-1/DECISIONS.md. Key selections:
- Employee pool/weights are explicit accepted company agreements, not an invented
  global payroll percentage. Funds must come from settled operating revenue.
- Weekly periods start Monday midnight New York; daily report is New York noon.
  Store UTC instants, calendar keys and actual capture/effective timestamps.
- Opening member grants use separately authorized issuance, not the initial bank
  reserve. Loan funding is reserve-funded, with a real receivable/history.
- J$90,000 is the strict reserve-danger boundary; no automatic rate/trade change.
- Tax uses the owner's whole-income brackets, not marginal brackets. Corrected
  example: J$1,600 at 2% = J$32. Trading gain is net realized gain, not sale gross.
- FIFO exact basis, buyer fees in basis and seller fees out of proceeds. Unknown
  legacy basis holds the affected assessment for reconciliation, not trading.
- Trading losses offset that week's trading gains, not wages/interest; no v1
  carryforward. Posted wages/deposit interest are income; wires, principal,
  capital, grants, redistribution and unrealized changes are not taxable wages.
- Partial tax collection uses only available transaction funds; keep unpaid tax
  separately. No forced sale, overdraft, existing-hold consumption or tax interest.
- Company payments require explicit member consent to immutable plan terms;
  cancellation stops future renewals. Missed charges do not pile up automatically.
- Full refunds need available company operating cash and a linked original charge;
  no automatic wage clawback. Recurrence/DST and refund rules are documented.
- Redistribution is equally allocated within an approved funded budget, obligations
  and headroom above the reserve warning boundary. No fixed allowance was added.
- The rate recommendation model needs explainable complete cash-flow projections;
  no unsupported low-reserve-equals-rate-hike rule and no automatic application.
- Effective rate changes create one linked mild, one-tick monetary_policy event
  affecting active stocks, not crypto. This is a design default that still needs
  engine/validation implementation; no stock-price overwrite or guaranteed return.
- Company Posts are REQUIRED Stage 2 work, including author/operator withdrawal
  and deletion. Only detailed Discussion remains deferred.

No questions were asked during development. Unspecified commercial amounts are
required versioned configuration, not silently approved live terms: initial Joe
Rate, loan catalog/limits/terms/rates/lending budget, service prices/intervals,
company employee pool/weights and redistribution budget. Until configured and
authorized, the relevant future handlers must refuse activation.
