    As described in section 3, subscriptions and purchases can increase the revenue of a 
    company. The statistics will include every essential data describing the current 
    weekly revenue, total revenue, weekly stock changes and the shareholding status
    of every shareholder, company value, and etc.

2 - Joe Banking update:
    Joe Banking will own an amount of money, controlling the total money supply and 
    money in the market. Updating each day at 12pm, the console will show the statistics
    of total money in the market, total asset in the market, average asset value of all
    players (should be floating around J$10,000, include change shown in percentage), tax
    revenue, bank reserved money, and all essential statistics used to control the money 
    circulation. 

3 - Money Circulation:
    Joe Trading uses a strict system for protecting a stable money supply. As a member
    joins the Joe Trading system, they receive J$10,000 as the starting disposable balance.
    A member can trade normally using existing functions. Here are new features:
        - Subscription and Spending Activities API: A company can launch services that allow
        subcribing. For example, ChatJoePT can direct users to a subscription link and allow
        subscription in ChatJoePT, creating income for the company ChatJoePT. A part of the 
        revenue is given to employers at the company, the rest of the revenue is contributed 
        to the company asset value or the stock price. 
        - Taxes: According to weekly income, a member will be taken away a portion of income into 
        the Joe Bank. Income is calculated in weekly income instead of monthly income. 
        In the range of J$0 to J$500, no tax is collected. In the range of J$500 to J$2,000, 2% tax
        is collected. In the range of J$2,000 to J$3,500, 3% tax is collected. In the range of 
        J$3,500 to J$5,000, 5% tax is collected. Any income above J$5,000 has 10% tax collected.
        Suppose a member gains J$1,000 throughout the week as income, and J$600 as
        stock exchange gain. The income is calculated as J$1000+600 = J$1,600 in
        total, corresponding to the category of J$500 to J$2,000, 2% is collected and
        transferred into the bank reserves, which is J$1,600 * 2% = J$80. 
        - Money supply stability and inequality control: There should be an additional mechanism to 
        redistribute the collected tax, and interest gained by the bank. The bank should start with 
        a reserve value of J$100,000, the total reserve value should be no lower than 90% of that 
        starting point, or it will be marked as dangerous. The system will calculate a recommended 
        interest rate change according to the remaining reserve amount. Final changes remain at the
        operator.
