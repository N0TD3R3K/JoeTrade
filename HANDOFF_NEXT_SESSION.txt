Existing authoritative rate rules:
Read Universe/joe-banking/docs/interest-policy-and-news.md first.
- Joe Rate is quoted PER WEEK, not APR.
- Resulting variable weekly rate must remain within 0.300%–2.000%.
- At most TWO successful effective adjustments per New York calendar week.
- "0.200% change" means 0.200 percentage points, not 20% relative growth.
  Example: 1.000% + 0.005 percentage points = 1.005% per week.
- Exact storage: weekly_rate_ppm; 0.005 percentage points = 50 ppm.
  Allowed nonzero signed changes are multiples of 50 ppm, absolute value
  50–2,000 ppm. Variable-rate bounds are 3,000–20,000 ppm.
- Require a rate-policy entitlement or banking administrator role, reason
  category, member-visible explanation, internal note and policy-version check.
  An exchange operator is not automatically a banking rate-policy operator.
- Never backdate an effective rate or rewrite accrued interest/history.
- Variable products use the new rate prospectively. Fixed contracts retain
  their accepted rates. A reversal after execution is a new adjustment and
  consumes another weekly allowance; it is not cancellation.
- Initial Joe Rate is still unconfirmed; 1.000% per week was only a proposal.

Scheduling design to resolve/implement next session:

  Decide how a date-only input maps to a time; do not silently schedule a past

Rate changes and stock-market news:
The owner's proposed direction is a reasonable simplified simulation rule:
- Rate cut -> bullish pressure.
- Rate increase -> bearish pressure.
Real equity reactions depend on expectations, reasons for the change and other
conditions; neither direction guarantees every stock will rise or fall.
Reference: Federal Reserve, transmission of monetary policy (April 22, 2025):
https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/kugler20250422a.htm

Preserve the existing architecture: a rate operation creates a visible,
attributed, structured Joe Wire event; it must not directly overwrite prices.
The market engine applies news alongside other inputs, hourly/daily caps and
market timing. The user now explicitly requests automatic event creation;
update the older optional/manual news wording to reflect that decision while
keeping the bank rate and market event as distinct, linked records.

Recommended timing, to make cancellation meaningful: publish the effective
market-impact event exactly once when the rate change successfully applies.
A scheduled announcement may be displayed earlier, but it must not apply the
same price effect again at execution. Record the selected timing policy.
Use durable delivery/retry protection between Banking and JoeSE so an outage
cannot lose or duplicate the event. Preserve a cancellation audit trail.
