- Switching Alice/Bob remounts member-specific financial/company views so the

- Static Stage 1 preview scripts remain explicitly static and do not connect the

B. Posts and company permissions
- Actual company-specific publishing APIs and form are implemented.
- Developer link or a positive settled stock/frozen holding qualifies. A pending
  buyer's share offer does not qualify. Insert-time checks enforce ownership.
- Author or authorized operator/moderator may withdraw/delete using the current
  post version. Former shareholders retain removal rights on their own posts.
- Withdrawal hides a post from other members. Deletion nulls title/body and keeps
  an attributed tombstone/action log. Idempotency records store hashes/responses,
  not an extra copy of deleted post text.
- Console Joe Bank includes a post moderation queue and reasoned removal controls.
- Discussion remains an honest planned page; no separate forum was invented.

C. My Company
- Actual developer/shareholder reports show weekly net and lifetime gross revenue,
  refunds, employee payments, retained revenue, operating cash, verified locked
  capital, market capitalization, prior-week stock comparison when evidence exists,
  and shareholder names/quantities/percentages.
- Shareholders receive aggregate data only. Billing/customer receipts, employee
  agreements and service management fields are returned only to Banking managers.
- Registered developers can initialize company banking metadata. Matched founding
  journals are attributed without moving or redeeming locked founding capital.
- Manager forms cover service requests, immutable plans, employee pool/agreements,
  funded payroll and full linked refunds. Private payment references are scoped.
- An unavailable company bank record or weekly close is labeled unavailable.
  Revenue never directly overwrites a stock price.

D. Approved policy and Banking ledger commands
- New commands bind a stable key to actor and canonical request hash. Stored
  responses replay after lost replies. Different terms with the same key conflict.
- A database revision guard serializes economy planning. Command, domain updates,
  balanced journal postings, audit records and outbox entries commit atomically.
- Approved policy versions, billing plan terms, accepted offer terms, income events,
  assessment revisions and audit records have additional immutability protections.
- Policy editor supports an explicit initial weekly Joe Rate, lending budget,
  reviewed fixed/variable loan catalog, redistribution budget and opening-grant gate.
  Draft and approval are separate actions. Existing accepted contracts retain terms.
- Reserve initialization posts J$100,000 exactly once against explicit issuance.
  Warning is strictly below J$90,000; no automatic seizure or price change.
- Eligibility requires an exact linked verified JoeAuth subject and review evidence.
  Existing J$10,000 grants can be reconciled to their original opening journals.
  New grants require approved eligibility, enabled policy and an empty new account;
  uniqueness protects both member and subject. Grants do not drain bank reserve.

E. Company payments, subscriptions and employees
- Service requests bind the company to a JoeAuth app; an operator approves billing.
- Immutable server-priced purchase/subscription plans require exact member consent.
  Cash moves from available member funds to company operating funds once.
- New York calendar-day recurrence handles DST gaps/repeated hours. Delayed jobs
  charge one period and skip missed intervals; no automatic catch-up pile.
- Insufficient funds or inactive/changed terms make a renewal past due. Cancellation
  stops future renewals. A member must explicitly retry a missed payment.
- Full refunds link the original settled payment, reverse its recognized revenue
  and use available company operating cash. An underfunded refund stays pending;
  one original payment has at most one refund. Posted wages are not clawed back.
- Employee pool and weights are explicit company configuration. Employees accept
  immutable agreement terms; accepted weights must total 100% before payroll.
- A closed revenue week has one funded payroll run, limited by settled net revenue,
  prior allocations and company cash. Largest-remainder allocation preserves cents.
  Employee income belongs to its actual posting week, not the older revenue week.

F. Exact gains, weekly tax and redistribution
- New acquisitions/disposals record exact FIFO basis by owner/asset/holding kind.
  Buyer fees enter acquisition cost; seller fees reduce realized proceeds.
- Founder basis is the actual contribution. Partial allocations conserve original
  basis, and final disposal consumes remaining cents.
- Existing quantities/sales import as explicit unresolved evidence when exact basis
  cannot be proven. Rounded average cost or today's quote is never substituted.
- Operator tools review remaining legacy lots and separately resolve historical
  sale income with evidence. Unknown affected weeks stay held for review.
- Income delivery advances its exchange cutoff under the existing market mutex,
  after settlement recovery. New sale time/proceeds match the actual bank journal.
- Closed-week tax applies the owner's WHOLE-income brackets. Wages/interest plus
  positive net realized trading gain are taxable; trading losses do not erase wages.
  Grants, redistribution, wires, principal, capital and unrealized value are excluded.
- The owner's arithmetic example is J$1,600 x 2% = J$32. Bracket jumps remain visible.
- Assessments record policy/evidence and explicit immutable revision chains.
  Collection uses available cash only, retains unpaid tax, and respects existing holds.
  Reviewed overpayments can be refunded from reserve without double refunding.
- Redistribution requires an operator-funded approved budget, received tax/interest,
  a recorded eligible member set, reserve headroom and retained tax-refund obligations.
  It is equal and cent-conserving; it does not mint a new allowance.

G. Rates, news, loans and scheduled work
- Console rate button opens a real date/change/reason/explanation dialog.
- Joe Rate is weekly 0.300%–2.000%. Changes are signed 0.005–0.200 percentage points,
  step 0.005, with two operations per New York week. Future slots are reserved;
  pending operations can be canceled, releasing their slots.
- Execution revalidates policy, expected rate version, sequence and actual-week quota.
  It records actual execution time and fails stale sequences rather than backdating.
- Applied changes and the durable event commit together. Exchange stores one receipt
  and one linked monetary_policy Joe Wire article. Cuts give a mild bullish model
  signal; increases a mild bearish one, for one eligible stock tick, excluding crypto.
  The original market caps still apply. Scheduling/cancellation produces no applied news.
  Internal notes remain private. News exposes effective time and operation reference.
- Approved catalog loan offers move no cash or interest until exact member acceptance.
  Acceptance requires initialized reserve, current terms, available reserve and budget.
  Variable starting offers must be refreshed if the index changes before acceptance.
- Disbursement is reserve-funded and records a principal receivable/obligation pair.
  Repayment pays interest first, then principal. Fixed terms remain fixed; variable
  accrual splits at actual rate boundaries with exact fractional-cent carry.
  Due time follows the accepted term; no unapproved penalty or forced sale was added.
- Banking's five-minute job handles bounded due rates, renewals, loan accrual/expiry,
  closed-week funded payroll, tax assessments/available collection and noon captures.
  Exchange's existing recovery job also synchronizes income and bank events.
  Local Wrangler does not automatically invoke cron; Stage 3 must test its handlers.

H. Noon dashboard, recommendations and service delivery
- Dashboard separates member posted/available/held cash, company operating cash,
  reserve, locked capital and fee clearing. Holds are not added twice.
- Noon observations show asset and portfolio averages separately, including the
  assets-versus-J$10,000 percentage. Missing evidence or no members gives unavailable.
- Bank-only captures are provisional. Shared captures compare bank financial/member
  checkpoints while the exchange is locked, then validate unchanged bank balances
  in the committing batch. Late capture times are honest; revisions are attributed.
- Operator-only rate recommendations explain seven-day variable-loan collectibility
  assumptions. Missing obligations/data gives insufficient data; nothing auto-applies.
- Approved merchant apps can pull only their own durable entitlement results with
  strict JoeAuth app tokens. Ordered results use JoeAuth subject and service-specific
  HMAC signatures, with separate idempotent acknowledgment from exchange receipts.
  The master signing secret is never sent to merchants or members.
- Banking and exchange live OpenAPI references and public member Guide are updated.
  The bank website Interest Rate and Loans pages now use real rate/loan/subscription
  APIs. Deposit funding and deposit interest remain unavailable.
